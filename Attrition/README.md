<h2>Predicting Employee Resignation</h2>

<h3>Overview</h3>
<p>
  Employee resignation prediction is a machine learning classification problem that identifies employees who are likely to leave an organization. This project uses <strong>Logistic Regression</strong>, a supervised learning algorithm, to predict whether an employee is likely to resign based on historical data such as age, job role, salary, years at the company, job satisfaction, work-life balance, and performance-related factors. The model estimates the probability of resignation and classifies employees as either likely to stay or resign, providing an interpretable and efficient solution for employee attrition analysis.
</p>

<h3>Application</h3>
<ul>
    <li>Predicting employee resignation helps organizations proactively improve workforce retention by identifying employees who may be at risk of leaving.</li>
    <li> Human resource teams can use these insights to implement targeted retention strategies, career development programs, compensation reviews, employee engagement initiatives, and workplace improvements.</li>
</ul>
